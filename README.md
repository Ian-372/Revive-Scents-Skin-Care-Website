# Revive Scents and Skin Care Solutions — Website

A responsive, static website foundation for the Revive brand.

## Files
- `index.html` — full page structure and content
- `style.css` — responsive visual design
- `script.js` — loader, mobile navigation, reveal effects, skin quiz and WhatsApp links
- `assets/brand-poster.jpg` — the client's supplied flyer used as a temporary brand visual

## Important
The product catalogue intentionally says **Coming Soon** until the real inventory is supplied. No fake products or prices were added.

When the stock list is ready, the next stage is:
1. Product database/catalogue
2. Product images
3. Real prices and stock quantities
4. Cart
5. Checkout
6. M-Pesa / payment integration
7. Delivery/collection options
8. Admin inventory management

## Research basis
General skin-care copy was kept conservative and aligned with dermatology guidance around skin types, moisturising, sunscreen and shaving/razor-bump care. This site is not a diagnostic or medical service.
