QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you ask,
we probably already did.

Can Revive recommend products for my skin type? +

Yes. The consultation flow is designed to understand your skin type, concerns, routine and goals before guiding you toward suitable options.

Do you cater for men too? +

Absolutely. Men's face care, shaving, beard-area care, body care and scents will have their own shopping categories.

Can I ask about a product before buying? +

Yes. WhatsApp is the quickest route while the online catalogue is being prepared. Send your concern or the product you are considering.

Will I be able to order online? +

Yes. The shop is intentionally marked Coming Soon until the real stock inventory, prices and delivery details are supplied. Payments can then be connected without replacing the whole site.

What if my skin problem is serious or painful? +

Everyday skincare can support a routine, but persistent, painful, rapidly changing or severe skin problems should be assessed by a qualified healthcare professional or dermatologist.
